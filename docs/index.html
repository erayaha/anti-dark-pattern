📋 Detection Rules & Regulatory Safeguards

confirm-shaming

Confirm Shaming Copy

Flags emotionally manipulative opt-out copy that shames users into accepting offers (FTC 16 CFR Part 425 compliance).

forced-continuity

Forced Continuity Messaging

Flags dark patterns that obscure automatic renewal dates and surprise post-trial charge flows.

hidden-costs

Hidden Cost Disclosures

Detects mandatory service and processing fees surfaced late in checkout instead of upfront.

countdown-urgency

Artificial Urgency & Scarcity

Catches unsubstantiated countdown timers and artificial scarcity copy designed to rush decisions.

roach-motel

Roach-Motel Cancellation Gates

Blocks multi-step friction and mandatory exit survey gates that prevent straightforward cancellation.

trick-question

Pre-selected / Read-Only Opt-ins

Detects pre-checked, non-interactive or read-only checkboxes that bypass explicit informed consent.

obstructive-consent

Obstructive Consent UI

Detects consent banners where "Accept All" is prominent while "Reject" is hidden via CSS or aria-hidden.
